### "Higala-AI" – Dialect-Aware Crisis First Responder & Psychological Support Web

Problem & Impact (30%): Youth mental health challenges, acute stress, anger
escalation, existential crises, and suicidal ideation are rising urban issues
in CDO. Yet, severe social stigma, long waitlists, and understaffed hotlines
prevent immediate support. Existing digital mental health tools often feel
clinical, robotic, or overly agreeable—failing to offer realistic personal
accountability while completely missing local Bisaya/Cebuano linguistic and
cultural nuances.

Solution & User Experience (40%): A 24/7 dialect-aware web application built
with Gemini 3 on Firebase Hosting. Designed around the spirit of true
friendship (Higalaay), it provides structured psychological tools tailored for
Kagay-anons:

Radical Candor ("No-Yes-Man" Agent): Unlike typical passive chatbots, Higala-AI
acts as a grounded, honest friend. It gently scolds users when they engage in
harmful behaviors, holds them accountable for poor choices, and challenges
toxic thought loops directly instead of enabling them.
